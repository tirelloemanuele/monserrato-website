About the Space

Nestled in the heart of Rome, this elegant apartment offers the perfect blend of historical charm and modern comfort. Located just steps away from iconic sites such as Piazza Navona, the Pantheon, and Campo de' Fiori, you'll find yourself immersed in the rich culture and vibrant energy of the Eternal City.

The apartment features beautiful decor with warm and inviting spaces. With three bedrooms, each with distinct character, and a spacious living room with a comfortable sofa, this apartment provides ample space for families or groups. The fully equipped kitchen includes a refrigerator, stove, and oven for preparing meals during your stay.

The two modern bathrooms are clean and well-maintained, with all necessary amenities including glass-enclosed showers, toilets, bidets, and sinks. Towels and bed linens are included for your convenience. This is a non-smoking accommodation, perfect for a comfortable stay in the heart of Rome.
